## Package 1: AI Foundations Workshop

**Best fit:** Broad employee, student, community, or workforce cohorts with mixed AI comfort levels.

**Core outcome:** Participants leave with practical AI literacy — they understand what current AI tools can and cannot do, have used at least two tools hands-on, and can identify one workflow they will improve with AI within a week.

**Standard scope (3–4 hours, one session):**
- Opening: AI landscape in plain language (30 min)
- Hands-on: prompt a useful output from scratch (45 min)
- Hands-on: automate a personal workflow (45 min)
- Guided discussion: responsible use, limitations, when not to use AI (30 min)
- Closing: each participant names their one next AI action (15 min)

**Expansion paths:**
- Add 2× one-hour office hours (virtual, within 2 weeks)
- Add a 90-minute follow-up lab one week later
- Extend to full-day with deeper hands-on builds

**Evidence to reference:** [Reference Client] engagement ($4,500), testimonials emphasizing scaffolded learning for beginners while engaging experienced participants.

**Customization levers (safe to adjust):**
- Industry-specific examples and prompts
- Duration: 2-hour condensed or full-day extended
- Delivery: in-person, virtual, or hybrid
- Audience size: scales well from 15 to 60+

**When to recommend:** Buyer says "we want our people to understand AI," "basic AI literacy," "getting started with AI," "AI for everyone." Audience is broad and non-technical.

## Package 2: AI Builder Lab

**Best fit:** Founders, operators, developers, creators, or innovation teams who want to build something real.

**Core outcome:** Every participant ships a working prototype, automated workflow, or repeatable build practice by the end of the session.

**Standard scope (full day, 6 hours):**
- Opening: the shift in software creation — what's now possible (30 min)
- Product thinking before building: problem, user, 3–5 features, starter prompt (45 min)
- Build sprint 1: scaffold and ship v0 (90 min)
- Working lunch / peer review (45 min)
- Build sprint 2: iterate, add one real feature (90 min)
- Demo + feedback: 3-min show-and-tell per participant/team (45 min)
- Closing: what to build next, resources, community invite (15 min)

**Expansion paths:**
- Cohort series (3–6 weekly sessions)
- Demo day for stakeholders or investors
- Train-the-trainer for internal facilitators

**Evidence to reference:** Testimonial themes — "walked away with a working prototype the same night," "completed a wishlist item in under 5 minutes," "now I feel like I can super easily make my ideas come alive."

**Customization levers (safe to adjust):**
- Build target: web app, internal tool, automation workflow, or creative project
- Duration: half-day sprint (3 hours) or two-day deep build
- Team size: individual, pairs, or small teams of 3–4
- Pre-work: send a 15-min setup guide so participants arrive with tools ready

**When to recommend:** Buyer says "we want our team to actually build something," "hands-on," "prototype," "ship faster," "founders who need to build." Audience has some technical comfort but may not be developers.

## Package 3: Team Adoption Sprint

**Best fit:** One functional team with a defined work problem they want to solve with AI.

**Core outcome:** The team identifies 3–5 shared AI use cases, establishes guardrails, and commits to a 30-day adoption plan with owner and success criteria.

**Standard scope (two half-days, typically one week apart):**

Day 1 (3 hours):
- Audit: current workflows and friction points (45 min)
- AI capability mapping: what tools solve which problems (30 min)
- Use case selection: prioritize 3–5 highest-impact, lowest-risk starting points (45 min)
- Guardrails workshop: data privacy, quality checking, when human review is required (45 min)
- Assignment: each person tests one use case before Day 2 (15 min)

Day 2 (3 hours):
- Show-and-tell: what worked, what didn't, what surprised you (45 min)
- Refine: solidify the 3–5 team use cases with prompts and process (60 min)
- Adoption plan: owner, timeline, success criteria, check-in cadence (45 min)
- Stakeholder brief: prepare a 5-min summary for leadership (30 min)

**Expansion paths:**
- Multi-team rollout (repeat for additional teams)
- Train-the-trainer: identify and equip internal champions
- 60- and 90-day check-ins with adoption metrics

**Evidence to reference:** Positioning — [Your Name] connects AI tools to durable product principles: user value, judgment, iteration, trust. Testimonial: "real frameworks, real examples, zero fluff."

**Customization levers (safe to adjust):**
- Team function: marketing, sales, operations, product, HR, etc.
- Duration: compressed single-day or extended three-session format
- Add executive summary session for leadership buy-in

**When to recommend:** Buyer says "our [marketing/ops/sales] team needs to adopt AI," "we need a plan," "we want to use AI but don't know where to start as a team," "governance and guardrails." Buyer has a specific team and problem.

## Package 4: Custom Program / Series

**Best fit:** Institution or program running repeat cohorts (university course, accelerator batch, workforce program cycle, annual training).

**Core outcome:** Curriculum tailored to audience, outcomes, and schedule — delivered as a repeatable program rather than a one-off.

**Standard scope:** Defined per engagement. Typical structures:

- **Semester course:** 8–12 weekly sessions, 90 min each, with office hours
- **Accelerator module:** 2–3 sessions embedded in existing cohort program
- **Workforce series:** 4–6 sessions over 4–6 weeks, with capstone project
- **Annual training:** 1–2 sessions per cohort, repeated 2–4× per year

**Expansion paths:**
- Annual partnership with recurring delivery
- Facilitator enablement: train internal staff to deliver
- Curriculum licensing with periodic updates

**Customization levers:** This IS the customization package. Start from Foundations or Builder Lab as the core module, then add layers.

**When to recommend:** Buyer says "we run cohorts," "this is part of a program," "we do this every [semester/quarter/year]," "can you teach our facilitators too." Multi-session, recurring, or institutional.

## Customization Decision Tree

When a buyer describes their need, follow this path:

1. **Does it match a proven package?** → Recommend that package with audience-specific tailoring. Do not invent new curriculum.

2. **Is it a minor variation?** (Different industry, audience size, delivery format, duration) → Adapt the closest package. This is normal customization, not scope creep.

3. **Is it a structural request?** (Multiple cohorts, train-the-trainer, curriculum licensing, multi-year) → This is Custom Program territory. Scope and price accordingly.

4. **Is it creating an entirely new workshop topic, format, or outcome that [Your Name] hasn't delivered?** → **FLAG AS OVER-CUSTOMIZATION.** Push back: "Here's what I've delivered successfully that's closest to what you're describing. Let's start there and adapt — it's faster, proven, and more affordable than building from scratch."
