# Follow-Up Email Templates

## Post-Discovery Call: Proposal Send

```text
Subject: Workshop proposal for [Organization] — [Package Name]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the conversation on [day]. Based on what you shared about [specific need / initiative / audience], I've put together a proposal for a [Package Name].

The short version:
- [Format, duration]
- [1–2 key outcomes]
- Proposed investment: $[X]–$[Y]

Full proposal and scope of work attached.

I'm available [day/time options] to walk through it. What works for you?

[Your Name]
```

## Proposal Follow-Up (3–5 Days, No Response)

```text
Subject: Re: Workshop proposal for [Organization]

Hi [Name],

Following up on the proposal I sent [day]. Happy to answer any questions or adjust the scope if something doesn't line up.

One thing I didn't mention: [one new piece of useful context — relevant testimonial, audience insight, timing hook, or related workshop outcome].

Let me know if a quick call would be helpful.

[Your Name]
```

## Second Follow-Up (7–10 Days, Still No Response)

```text
Subject: One thought on [audience / initiative]

Hi [Name],

One last thought — [concrete insight tied to their audience or initiative that shows you understand their world]. If the timing isn't right, I completely understand. I'd rather be useful than persistent.

If this isn't the right moment, is there a better time to revisit, or someone else on your team I should connect with?

[Your Name]
```

## Post-Proposal Discussion: Recap and Confirmation

```text
Subject: Recap and next steps — [Organization] workshop

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the conversation today. Here's what we aligned on:

- **Program:** [Package Name]
- **Dates:** [Date(s)]
- **Audience:** [Description and approx. count]
- **Format:** [In-person / virtual / hybrid]
- **Fee:** $[X] ([deposit/balance terms])

Next step: I'll send the contract by [date]. Once that's signed and the deposit is in, we'll lock the dates and I'll start prep.

In the meantime, here's what would be helpful from your side:
- [Action item 1]
- [Action item 2]

Excited to work with your team.

[Your Name]
```

## Verbal Commit Confirmation

```text
Subject: Great — [Organization] workshop confirmed

Hi [Name],

Great to hear we're moving forward. Here's what happens next:

1. I'll send the contract and deposit invoice by [date].
2. Once the deposit is received ([50% = $X]), the dates are locked and I begin prep.
3. I'll schedule a brief prep call for [proposed timeframe] to finalize audience details and materials.

Looking forward to it.

[Your Name]
```

## Post-Workshop Thank You + Invoice

```text
Subject: Thank you — [Organization] workshop + invoice

Hi [Name],

Thank you for having me work with your team yesterday. [One specific positive observation from the session].

I've attached the balance invoice. I'd also love a brief debrief when you have a moment — what landed, what could be better, and what outcomes you're seeing from participants.

If the session was valuable, a short testimonial or an introduction to a peer organization running similar programs would mean a lot.

[Your Name]
```

## Stalled Deal: Re-engagement (3+ Weeks, Warm Signal)

```text
Subject: [Organization] — still thinking about [audience/initiative]?

Hi [Name],

I came across [relevant article / program / funding announcement / event] and it reminded me of our conversation about [topic].

If AI education is still on your radar for [timeframe], I'd love to pick the conversation back up. No pressure — just didn't want to lose touch.

[Your Name]
```
