# Deposit and Invoice Guide

## Standard Payment Structure

| Payment | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit | 50% of total fee | On contract signing, to confirm dates |
| Balance | 50% of total fee | On workshop delivery or net-15 after |

## Deposit

### Purpose
Confirms the date and covers prep work. Non-refundable deposit is standard — it protects against last-minute cancellations after [Your Name] has invested prep time.

### Deposit Invoice Template

```
INVOICE

From: [Your Company]
Contact: [your-email@yourcompany.com]

To: [Organization Name]
Attn: [Contact Name]
Email: [Contact Email]

Invoice #: [ORG]-DEP-[YYYYMMDD]
Date: [Date]
Due: On receipt

Description: 50% deposit for [Package Name] workshop
Dates: [Workshop date(s)]
Amount: $[X]

Payment method: [ACH / wire / check — instructions below]

Thank you.
```

### Deposit Confirmation

When deposit is received:
- [ ] Confirm receipt to the client via email
- [ ] Confirm the workshop date is locked
- [ ] Begin prep work

## Payment Methods

[Your Name] should confirm which methods are available. Common options:

- **ACH / bank transfer:** Preferred — lowest fees
- **Check:** Acceptable but slower; factor in mail + clearing time
- **Credit card:** Only if [Your Name] has a payment processor set up (e.g., Stripe)
- **Wire transfer:** For international clients

If [Your Name] uses Stripe, include a payment link.

## Follow-Up on Unpaid Invoices

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| Day of delivery | Send balance invoice |
| 3 days past due | Friendly reminder: "Wanted to make sure the invoice landed in the right inbox. Let me know if you need anything from me." |
| 10 days past due | Direct follow-up: "Following up on invoice #[N] sent on [date]. Please let me know when we can expect payment or if there's an issue on your end." |
| 21 days past due | Escalate to decision-maker or finance contact: "Checking in on invoice #[N], now three weeks past due. Can you connect me with the right person in accounts payable?" |
| 30+ days past due | Flag to [Your Name] for direct intervention |
