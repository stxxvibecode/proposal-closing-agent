# Contract Handoff Checklist

Use when a deal reaches verbal commit. Adapt to the specific engagement — not all items apply to every deal.

## Before Sending the Contract

- [ ] Final scope confirmed in writing (email is sufficient)
- [ ] Fee agreed — total and payment structure
- [ ] Dates locked
- [ ] Format confirmed (in-person / virtual / hybrid)
- [ ] Audience size range confirmed
- [ ] Travel and expenses addressed (if applicable)
- [ ] Cancellation / rescheduling terms discussed
- [ ] Recording and usage rights clarified (if applicable)
- [ ] Intellectual property: who owns materials, slides, recordings
- [ ] Deposit amount and due date confirmed
- [ ] Final invoice due date confirmed
- [ ] Point of contact for contract review identified

## Contract Essentials

The contract (or statement of work) should cover:

- [ ] Parties: [Your Company] / [Your Name] and [Organization]
- [ ] Scope of work (reference the SOW document)
- [ ] Fee and payment schedule
- [ ] Deposit: [50% standard — amount, due date, non-refundable or refundable terms]
- [ ] Balance: [due on delivery / net-15 / net-30]
- [ ] Dates and duration
- [ ] Format and location
- [ ] Audience (description, not exact headcount guarantee unless required)
- [ ] Cancellation policy: [e.g., 14+ days notice = full refund less deposit; 7–13 days = 50%; <7 days = no refund]
- [ ] Rescheduling: [e.g., one reschedule within 60 days at no charge if 14+ days notice]
- [ ] Force majeure
- [ ] Intellectual property: [Your Name] retains IP in materials and methodology; organization gets a license to use materials internally
- [ ] Recording rights: [if applicable — term, channels, edit rights, ownership]
- [ ] Limitation of liability
- [ ] Governing law: [typically New York]
- [ ] Signatures: both parties

## After Contract Sent

- [ ] Follow up within 2 business days if no response
- [ ] Flag any requested redlines or changes
- [ ] Once signed: send deposit invoice immediately
- [ ] Confirm receipt of deposit before beginning prep

## Red Flags in Contract Review

- [ ] **IP assignment:** Organization wants to own all materials. Counter: license to use internally, [Your Name] retains IP.
- [ ] **Non-compete:** Overly broad non-compete restricting [Your Name]'s ability to work with other organizations. Counter: narrow to direct competitors for a limited time, or remove.
- [ ] **Indemnification:** One-sided indemnification. Counter: mutual where possible, or capped at fee amount.
- [ ] **Payment terms beyond net-30:** Net-60 or longer. Counter: net-30 or deposit + net-15.
- [ ] **Unlimited recording / distribution rights without additional fee.** Counter: define term, channels, and fee separately.
